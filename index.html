The time scale is compressed before 2019 and expanded afterwards, because releases are far denser in recent years. Dates are approximate first public release or publication; many models and papers are omitted. The parameter overlay uses the left log-scale axis, which is independent of the timeline axis in the middle. Each line is the running maximum of total parameters (MoE counted by total, not active) among models whose size is publicly disclosed. Commercial = closed-weight models; sizes of GPT-4 and later closed models are undisclosed, so that line stops being meaningful after early 2023. Open-weight = downloadable weights; research-only sparse checkpoints such as Google Switch-C (1.6T) and region-specific research models are excluded. Largest dense open model: Llama 3.1 405B. 2026 figures (DeepSeek-V4 Pro) come from secondary reports and should be verified. Parameter counts are totals (active parameters shown for MoE models); "undisclosed" means the developer has not published the size. Embedding and non-LLM entries show no count.
